Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Marklet ("the Extension") is built with a strict privacy-first, least-privilege architecture.

1. Data Collection & Network Activity

  • Zero data collection by Marklet: Marklet operates without a backend server and does not collect, track, analyze, or transmit your browsing history, page content, comments, or screenshots to any Marklet or third-party server.
  • Zero analytics or ads: Marklet contains no telemetry, tracking pixels, remote scripts, or third-party SDKs.
  • Optional Sign-In (Microsoft / Google): Signing in is completely optional. Only when you explicitly click Microsoft or Google in the extension popup does Marklet authenticate directly with Microsoft or Google using standard OAuth 2.0 to load your display name, email, and profile avatar into your local browser storage.
  • Private feedback links: When you click Send, Marklet packages your text comments locally inside a URL fragment so you can paste them into your own chat or email app. URL fragments are never sent to web servers.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • Comments, replies, optional display profile, and cropped element screenshots are stored locally on your device using your browser's built-in chrome.storage.local API.
  • You remain in full control at all times: sign out, export JSON or a Design Brief .zip, clear a single page, or clear all stored data directly from the extension menu.

3. Permissions Used

  • activeTab: Grants temporary access to the active tab only when you explicitly click Marklet and choose Start Review. Marklet never reads or modifies pages in the background.
  • scripting: Injects Marklet's isolated Shadow DOM commenting overlay into the active tab after you start a review.
  • storage: Saves your comments, element anchors, snapshots, and optional profile locally in your browser.
  • identity (and Microsoft / Google OAuth endpoints): Used only when you explicitly click Microsoft or Google to load your name, email, and avatar locally.

4. Contact

Questions or security reports? Please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
