Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026

Data collection

Zero data collection by Marklet. Marklet operates without a backend server and never collects, tracks, or transmits your browsing history, page content, comments, or snapshots to any Marklet or third-party server.

Zero analytics or ads. The extension contains no telemetry, tracking pixels, remote scripts, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Local storage & feedback links

Comments, replies, element snapshots, and optional profile details are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. You can export or clear your data at any time from the extension menu.

When you click Send, Marklet encodes your text comments into a URL fragment so you can paste a feedback link into Teams, Slack, or email. URL fragments stay in your browser and are not sent to web servers.

Optional sign-in

Signing in with Microsoft or Google is completely optional. When you choose to sign in, Marklet authenticates directly with Microsoft or Google via OAuth 2.0 using read-only profile scopes to display your name and avatar on comments.

Browser permissions

activeTab & scripting are used only after you explicitly click Start Review on the current tab. Marklet never reads or modifies webpages in the background.

storage saves your comments locally, and identity is used only if you initiate optional Microsoft or Google sign-in.

Contact

For privacy questions or security reports, please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
